Case study: Carehub Ops

Amazon.co.za seller

The problem

Carehub is my own Amazon.co.za store. Every week went to the same manual jobs: creating listings, chasing brand approvals, asking buyers for reviews, checking which ads paid back, and spotting lost stock to claim from Amazon.

What I built

  • A listing agent that creates listings and follows up brand approvals
  • Automatic review requests for every order
  • An ad report that shows where ad money pays back and where it doesn't
  • A stock check that flags lost or damaged inventory for claims

All four share one database, so the numbers always agree.
